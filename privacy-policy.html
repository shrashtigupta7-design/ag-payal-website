Last updated: July 2026

1. Who we are

AG Payal Pvt. Ltd. ("we", "us", "our") is a manufacturer of anklet designs based in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India. We work exclusively with business buyers — wholesalers, distributors, and do not sell to individual consumers through this website.

2. Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly when you contact us, such as through our enquiry form, email, or WhatsApp. This may include:

  • Your name and business name
  • Phone number, WhatsApp number, and email address
  • Business type and approximate order quantity
  • Any details you share about the designs or custom requirements you're interested in

We do not use cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics that identify individual visitors on this site, and we do not process any payment or financial information through this website.

3. How we use your information

We use the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry, prepare quotations, discuss your order, and, if you proceed, to fulfil and communicate about that order. We do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. How your enquiry reaches us

Messages sent through WhatsApp links on this site go directly to our WhatsApp Business number. Messages sent through "Email us" links or our contact form open your own email client — we do not receive form submissions through this website's server, and no enquiry data is stored on this site itself.

5. Data retention

We retain business correspondence (emails, WhatsApp messages) for as long as reasonably necessary to manage our relationship with you, respond to enquiries, and meet any legal or accounting obligations.

6. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by contacting us at hello@agpayal.in.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect the most recent revision.

8. Contact us
