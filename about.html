OUR STORY

A Family Craft, Scaled For Business

AG Payal Pvt. Ltd. began as a small family workshop in Agra, producing anklets using traditional metalworking techniques passed down through generations of artisans.

As demand grew from retailers and exporters, we built dedicated production capacity while keeping every design hand-finished — combining the consistency a bulk buyer needs with the detail only skilled hands can deliver.

Today, we manufacture exclusively for business buyers — no retail counter, no single-piece sales — so our full capacity goes toward serving wholesale and export partners well.
